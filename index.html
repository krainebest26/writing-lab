Show, Don't Tell: the ABSDT camera

A telling sentence gives the reader a fact. A showing sentence lets the reader see it and work out the feeling. Examiners give the most Content marks to the moment you slow down and show. Five letters help you do it.

Telling (Band 3)

I found a wallet with a lot of money and I was tempted to keep it.

Showing (Band 4 to 5)

A thick stack of red hundred-dollar notes stared back at me. My breath caught in my throat. My fingers tightened around the wallet.

Five kinds of showing sentence

SSensesWhat did I see, hear, smell or feel?
DDialogueWhat did someone say?
TThoughtWhat went through my mind?
